A blind-accessible acoustic stealth extraction game. Playable 100% via audio or with amber CRT oscilloscope.

  • Left Thumb / WASD: Drag the virtual joystick to steer your explorer in 360 degrees.
  • CLAP Button / Space: Emits an active echolocation pulse. Returns 24 directional stereo reflections.
  • SNEAK Button / Shift: Cuts movement noise from 175 px down to 45 px.
  • Water Hazard: Splashing in shallow water amplifies your footstep noise by 1.5x, alerting sound-hunting automatons!
  • Relic Chimes: Follow the directional stereo triad chimes to locate and collect all 3 relics.
  • Blind Mode (B): Turns the screen 100% black for pure audio immersion.
