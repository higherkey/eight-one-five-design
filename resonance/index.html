FIELD MANUAL

You stand in drowned ruins, in total darkness. You see and hear only what sound reveals. Sighted and blind players get the same cues: every echo is drawn as points of light, played in 3D audio, and captioned. Headphones help.

  • Move: W and S walk, A and D step sideways. Turn: the arrow keys, the mouse (click the view first), or drag on touch. Q and E turn exactly 45 degrees and say your new facing.
  • Clap (Space): each direction answers with a tap. A later tap means a farther wall, and silence means open passage. A drip means water, a crunch means gravel, and a metallic ring is an automaton. A deep boom (drawn in violet) is a hollow wall.
  • Knock (F): knock on the wall in front of you. Solid stone thuds. A hollow wall crumbles open, but the crash brings automatons running.
  • Bells: three relic bells chime from hidden niches, some sealed behind hollow walls. Walk into a bell to take it. Whoever finds a bell carries it.
  • The sealed door hums. Clap beside it and it sings a four-note melody. With all three bells found, ring them in that order beside the door (keys 1 low, 2 middle, 3 high). Every ring is loud. In the view, a higher light means a higher bell.
  • Escape: once the door opens, follow its beacon out.
  • Sneak (hold Shift, or C to toggle): slow and quiet. Water and gravel make every step carry farther.
  • Status (R) speaks your bells, the door's melody and your facing. Blind mode (B) turns the screen fully black.

Online, the ruins keep running while this help is open.
