Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 14, 2026

1. Respecting User Agency

At eight1five design, we believe your data belongs to you. In alignment with our core pillar of Subtle Royalty (Absolute User Agency), we do not track your browsing habits, utilize third-party marketing cookies, or build user behavior profiles.

2. Contact Form Inquiries

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect your name, email, subject, and message content solely to respond to your inquiry. This data is handled in confidence and is never sold, traded, or shared with third parties.

3. Data Retention

We retain email inquiry records only as long as necessary to complete our business communications. You may request the deletion of your message logs at any time by contacting us directly.

4. Cookies and Session Storage

This website does not use tracking cookies. We utilize standard local storage (`localStorage`) to remember your preferred visual theme choice (Structural vs. Authoritative). No personal data is captured.
