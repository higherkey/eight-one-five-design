Data Protection & Terms

Privacy Commitments

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Respecting User Agency

At eight1five design, we believe your data belongs to you. In alignment with our core pillar of absolute user agency, we do not track your browsing habits, utilize third-party marketing cookies, or build user behavior profiles.

2. Contact Form Inquiries

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect your name, email, subject, and message content solely to respond to your inquiry. This data is handled in strict confidence and is never sold, traded, or shared with third parties.

3. Data Retention

We retain email inquiry records only as long as necessary to complete our business communications. You may request the deletion of your message logs at any time by contacting us directly at isaac@eight1fivedesign.com.

4. Cookies and Session Storage

This website does not use tracking cookies. We utilize standard local storage (`localStorage`) solely to remember visual UI preferences. No personal identification data is ever captured or sent to external servers.
