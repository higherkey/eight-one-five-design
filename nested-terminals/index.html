Field manual

  • Move: WASD / arrow keys, or the on-screen pad (tap and hold, or focus a pad button and hold Enter/Space).
  • Interact (E / Space): jack into a wall terminal, or pull a floor lever.
  • Inside a terminal: flip inverters (tap, or keys 1–4) until the DOOR + LASER output lights. You are frozen and see the floor only as a minimap. Esc disconnects.
  • Every door output also arms that chamber's laser grid. Live beams cycle on and off: time your crossing, or have your partner hold the circuit dark until you are past.
  • Floor levers feed power into a circuit. No lever, no door.
  • Sentries sweep a loop. Get caught (or touch a live beam) and you reboot at the chamber start.
  • Reach EXTRACT to win. Co-op: everyone must extract. If the server is asleep you play solo offline; reload to try online again.
